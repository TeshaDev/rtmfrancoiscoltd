Full-service capabilities

One team covers the build and the trades that finish it, so you deal with a single contractor.

General construction

Site clearing, earthworks and site preparation, then foundations, structure, walls, slabs and roofing, taking your build from the first groundwork to a finished building.

Aluminium

Aluminium windows, sliding doors, partitions, shopfronts and railings, measured, made and fitted with durable frames and glazing.

Painting

Interior and exterior painting with careful surface preparation and protective coatings for homes and commercial buildings.

Renovation & extension

Renovation of houses, commercial buildings and schools, plus extensions and repairs.

False ceiling

Gypsum and PVC false ceilings, cornices and integrated lighting for a clean, finished interior.

Plumbing

Water supply, drainage, sanitary fittings and pipework installed, repaired and tested to standard.

Welding

Steel fabrication and welding for gates, railings, frames and structural elements, made to measure and installed on site.

Electrical, plastering, tiling & joinery

Electrical installation, plastering, tiling and joinery to complete the inside of your building.

Finishing & fit-out

Wall and floor coverings, interior and exterior fit-out, and end-of-works cleaning so the site is handed over clean.

Moving

Moving of houses, offices and shops.
